Lecture 1 · Tuesday 9/29

Why roll dice to do math?

Pose 1✦

Monte Carlo, in one breath

Answer a hard question by simulating random outcomes many times and summarizing what happened. The method got its name from the Monte Carlo casino, coined by Ulam and von Neumann in the 1940s.

Pose 2✦

Frequency becomes probability

Repeat an experiment n times. The share of times an event happens settles toward its probability as n grows. That's the Law of Large Numbers doing the work.

more draws → less wobble

Pose 3✦

“Random” on a computer

Software makes pseudo-random numbers: a deterministic sequence that behaves like independent Unif(0, 1) draws. Fix the start with set.seed() and your results reproduce exactly.

Pose 4✦

The quarter, in one frame

Uniforms → any distribution (inverse CDF, rejection) → estimates (importance sampling) → Markov chains → MCMC (Metropolis–Hastings, Gibbs).

Lecture 2 · Thursday 10/1

Introduction to Bayesian statistics

Pose 1✦

Two ways to see a parameter

Frequentist: the parameter θ is a fixed unknown; only the data are random.

Bayesian: uncertainty about θ is itself described by a probability distribution, which data then update.

Pose 2✦

Bayes' rule

p(θ | x) = p(x | θ) · p(θ)p(x)

Prior p(θ): belief before data. Likelihood p(x | θ): how well each θ explains the data. Posterior p(θ | x): updated belief.

posterior ∝ likelihood × prior

Pose 3✦

Beta meets Binomial

Coin with heads-probability θ. Prior Beta(a, b), then k heads in n flips gives

θ | data ~ Beta(a + k, b + n − k)

Same family before and after: a conjugate prior. Posterior mean (a + k)/(a + b + n).

Pose 4✦

The catch → this course

The denominator p(x) = ∫ p(x | θ) p(θ) dθ rarely has a closed form, and posterior summaries like E[θ | x] are more integrals.

Solution: draw samples from the posterior and average them. Every method this quarter is a way to do that.

Why the Monte Carlo number matters

Here the posterior mean has a formula, so you can check that averaging 5,000 posterior draws lands right next to it. In most real models there's no formula, and the draws are all you have.
